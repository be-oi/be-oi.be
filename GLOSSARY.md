# beOI contest

The public contest of the Belgian Olympiad in Informatics: its stages, and the difference between signing up to take part and joining the mailing list.

## Language

**Brain Teaser Round**:
The optional first stage: logic puzzles, no programming. A score here never decides who advances. Public names: Brain Teaser Round, Épreuve logique, Hersenkraker, Denksport-Runde.
_Avoid_: qualification round, initial round

**Qualification Rounds**:
The three independent online rounds (December, January, and February) through which a participant qualifies for the next stage. Passing one is enough. Public names: Qualification Rounds, Qualifications, Kwalificatierondes, Qualifikationsrunden.
_Avoid_: qualification round (singular — that was the former name of the Brain Teaser Round)

**Contest platform**:
The site at contest.be-oi.be, where contest registration happens.
_Avoid_: this website, be-oi.be

**Contest registration**:
Signing up on the contest platform to take part in the Brain Teaser Round and the Qualification Rounds. The school registers its pupils. For a participant taking part from home, a parent or guardian registers them.
_Avoid_: mailing list, newsletter, inscription on be-oi.be, self-registration

**Registration window**:
The period during which contest registration is open. It ends when the Qualification Rounds end.
_Avoid_: mailing-list signup

**Mailing list**:
The email list on be-oi.be for news about the olympiad.
_Avoid_: contest registration, registration
