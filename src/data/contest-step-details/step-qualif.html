How to join

Register on the beOI platform and pick any of the three rounds that fit your schedule. You can attempt more than one round if you want more practice.

Task type

Short programming and concept checks adapted to your age category. Supported environments include Blockly, Scratch, and Python.

Practical organization

Three independent online windows (typically December, January, and February). Each round lasts about 40 minutes and can be taken from school or home as announced.

Qualify for the next rounds

Passing any single round is enough to qualify for the quarter-final. Extra rounds are for learning — they are not required once you have already passed.

What you should know

Basic ideas for your age group: reading a short problem, writing a simple program, and checking that it works on sample cases.
