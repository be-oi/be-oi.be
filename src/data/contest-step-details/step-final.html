How to join

By personal invitation only after the semi-final. Travel and venue details are sent to finalists privately and their schools or parents.

Task type

Mid-level to very hard olympiad-style problems. Typically C++ and Python are the main languages used at this stage.

Practical organization

Usually two sessions of about three hours each, on site at a national venue in May. Full contest atmosphere with ranking and awards.

Qualify for the next rounds

National titles are awarded by age group. Strong results also feed into the longer path toward international team selection.

What to expect

Tough problems, a focused schedule, and a chance to meet other top competitors from across Belgium.
