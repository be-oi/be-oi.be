How to join

By personal invitation only after the semi-final. Travel and venue details are sent privately to finalists and their schools or parents.

Task type

Mid-level to very hard olympiad-style problems. Typically C++ and Python are the main languages used at this stage. C++ is strongly recommended, because it is often required (sometimes the only accepted language) at international contests.

Practical organization

Usually two sessions of about three hours each on the same day, on site at a national venue in May. Full contest atmosphere with ranking and awards. Participants may bring their own non-programmable keyboard if they wish, but AZERTY keyboards will be provided.

The competition will take place on the computers provided. Students may bring blank paper and writing materials. Further practical information will be provided to participants.

Awards

National titles and prizes are awarded by age group. Strong results also feed into the longer path toward international team selection.

What to expect

Challenging olympiad-style problems, a distinct competition schedule and atmosphere, and a chance to meet other top competitors from across Belgium.
