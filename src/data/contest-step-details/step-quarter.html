How to join

Open to contestants who passed a qualification round. You will receive instructions and a time slot through the contest platform and your school contact.

Task type

A set of basic programming problems: implement clear solutions, handle input/output, and reason about simple algorithms. More languages are allowed than in qualification (including Java and C++).

Practical organization

About two hours in March. Usually organised at school or under the remote/on-site rules published for that year.

Qualify for the next rounds

Ranking and age-category thresholds decide who advances to the semi-final. Aim for correct solutions under time pressure — partial scores may still help.

Preparation tips

Practise short contest-style tasks, get comfortable with your chosen language, and learn to test your code carefully before submitting.
