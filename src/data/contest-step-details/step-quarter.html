How to join

Open to contestants who passed a qualification round. You will receive instructions after qualifications.

Task type

A set of basic programming problems: implement clear solutions, handle input/output, and reason about simple algorithms. More languages are allowed than in qualification (including Java and C++). Some sample tasks will follow a bit later.

Practical organization

Two hours on a Wednesday in March. Can be done at school or at home. It is an online contest that only requires a browser and an Internet connection. Help from AI or from another person is not allowed. More details will follow.

Qualify for the next rounds

A fixed number of contestants will qualify in each age category. You will know whether you have qualified a few days after the contest.

Preparation tips

Practise short contest-style tasks, get comfortable with your chosen language, and learn to test your code carefully before submitting.
