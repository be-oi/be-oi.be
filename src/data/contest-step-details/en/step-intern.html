How to join

There is no open registration. Selection is based on sustained results across national contests.

Task type

International olympiad problems — typically in C++ — at the level of WEOI, EJOI, EGOI, and IOI, depending on the event and age track.

Practical organization

Training during the year, training camps and selection contests around the summer, then travel to the host country of each international olympiad. Exact calendars change every year.

Being a member of the selection pool requires a significant commitment spanning several years.

Selection path

Coaches look at a track record, not a single contest. Consistency, growth, and performance in training all matter.

Team Belgium

Selected students represent Belgium abroad, with support from beOI for preparation and logistics as announced each season.
