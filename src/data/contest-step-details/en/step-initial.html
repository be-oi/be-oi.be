How to join

The challenge will be open during a full week at the end of November for teachers, so they can run it with their students. After that week, the challenge will be open for a few days to anyone participating from home. More details will follow — make sure you subscribe to our mailing list to be informed when it opens.

Task type

Only logic puzzles involving computational thinking. No actual programming — neither block-based programming nor a code editor.

How to train

Try last year’s tasks on concours.be-oi.be, in the “s'entraîner” section (tasks available in English). The first five tasks are typical of what you may encounter. More training tasks will be provided later.

Practical organization

About 45 minutes, around the end of November. The exam takes place online: you need a computer with a web browser and a basic Internet connection. Supervised at school or under the rules announced for home participation that year. More details will follow.

Not required to advance

You may skip this round and still join the rest of beOI. Your score has no effect on who qualifies — advancement starts with the Qualification Rounds. This stage is only a friendly first look at computational thinking.
