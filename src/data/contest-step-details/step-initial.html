How to join

Open to all pupils who want to try — no prior registration beyond the usual contest signup. Schools can host a session, or you can take part from home if your school organises it that way.

Task type

Multiple-choice and short-answer logic puzzles: patterns, reasoning, and computational thinking. No programming language, no code editor.

Practical organization

About 45 minutes, typically at the end of November. Supervised at school or under the rules announced for home participation that year.

Qualify for the next rounds

This stage is optional fun only. Results do not count toward qualification for later stages of beOI.

Who it is for

Especially useful for younger students and anyone curious about problem-solving before they start coding contests.
