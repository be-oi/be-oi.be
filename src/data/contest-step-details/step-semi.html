How to join

Invitation after the quarter-final. You will be assigned to a trusted school or a regional center; bring what the invitation asks for (ID, login details, etc.).

Task type

Harder algorithmic problems: data structures, careful implementation, and edge cases. Still within reach for strong contestants in each age group.

Practical organization

About three hours on site in April. Supervised rooms, fixed start time, and the standard contest rules for devices and communication.

Qualify for the next rounds

Be among the best in your age group. The top performers advance to the national final according to the published cut-offs.

On the day

Arrive early, follow the supervisors’ instructions, and manage your time across problems — it is better to fully solve a few than to leave many unfinished.
