How to join

Personal invitation after the quarter-final. You will be assigned to a trusted school or a regional center; bring what the invitation asks for (ID, login details, etc.).

Organize the contest in my school

We are looking for schools that can host the contest in their building at the same time as the other regional centers (a Wednesday, 2–5 pm). The requirements are basically computers with a web browser and an Internet connection. We ask organizers to supervise contestants carefully and ensure they do not communicate with others (online or in the room) and do not use AI to solve the tasks. If your school can help, contact us at info@be-oi.be and tell us whether you can also welcome students from outside your school.

Task type

Harder algorithmic problems: data structures, careful implementation, and edge cases. Still within reach for committed contestants in each age group.

Practical organization

About three hours on site in April. Supervised rooms, fixed start time, and the standard contest rules for devices and communication.

Manage your time across problems — it is better to fully solve a few than to leave many unfinished.

Qualify for the next rounds

Rank-based selection. The top performers advance to the national final according to the announced cut-offs.
