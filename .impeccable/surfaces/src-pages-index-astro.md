# Language picker

Scope: root `/`, shown when the browser language is not French, Dutch, or German, and when `?choose` is present.
Mode: operate.
Audience: visitors who must pick Nederlands, Français, Deutsch, or English.
Job: recognize beOI from the mark and the official name, then enter.
Constraints: auto-redirect unchanged; no English-only introduction; no equal language cards; the real logo; language names smaller than the official name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The large beOI mark proves the site before a language is chosen. The page refuses equal language cards and display-size language titles.

OWN-WORLD: Contest-blue ink on craft paper. The real logo owns the left half, with no separate wordmark. One vertical rule separates it from a ruled list: a small language name, the official name, and a full pill of the same weight. Flat. No cards.

STORY: A visitor whose language was not detected recognizes beOI from the mark and from the official name in a language they know, then enters with that language’s button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a wide screen the left half is the real logo at roughly a third of the viewport height. There is no wordmark beside it. The right half is four rows. Each row leads with the native language name, then the official name at body size, then a short button that does not repeat the language. Nothing is pre-selected. On a narrow screen the logo stacks above the list, still the largest element. Signature interaction, Ink mark: pointing or focusing a row turns that row’s language label and rule Contest Blue without enlarging the label.

FORM: Split mast, fifth grounded structure, seed eae52f30.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
